The method

The click marks the moment. The treat makes it worth it.

A click is the same sharp sound every time, so your dog learns exactly which thing earned the reward. Every Pawfer lesson is built on this loop of positive reinforcement.

  1. 1The cue

    Ask

    Say “Sit” once, then wait. Give your dog a moment to work it out.

  2. click!
    2The instant

    Click

    Click the moment it happens, the second their bottom touches the floor. Not after.

  3. 3Within a second

    Treat

    Every click earns a treat, quickly. That promise is what makes the click mean something.

  4. 4About five minutes

    Repeat

    A handful of reps, then stop while it’s still fun. Come back tomorrow and build on it.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in is optional, with Apple or Google. It backs up your dog’s progress so you can restore it on a new phone. Without an account, everything stays on your device.

Is it for puppies or older dogs?

Both. Six quick questions about your dog and your goals pick the first program. Puppy start covers the potty routine, crate comfort and happy handling.

Do I need a physical clicker?

No. The clicker is built in, with Classic, Soft and Whistle sounds and a haptic on every tap.

How do I cancel Pawfer Plus?

In your App Store account settings, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting your Pawfer account doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Pawfer is on iPhone first.
