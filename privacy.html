Pawfer ("we", "us") is a dog-training app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

You can use Pawfer without an account

Signing in is optional. Without an account, your dog's profile and training progress stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Information we collect

  • Account details (only if you sign in). When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and, if you share it, your name. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead.
  • Training progress (only if you sign in). Your dog's name, breed, age, goals, completed lessons, streak and app settings, so we can back them up and restore them on another device.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Pawfer Plus, Apple or Google processes the payment. We never see your card details. We receive a purchase record (product, dates, status) so we can unlock Plus for you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone audio, and we do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and back up and restore your progress.
  • To unlock and restore Pawfer Plus.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data for us:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and progress backup (data stored in Singapore).
  • RevenueCat: your account ID (if you sign in) and purchase records, so Pawfer Plus follows you to a new phone.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in and in-app payments, under their own privacy policies.

Where your data is stored and how we protect it

Backed-up data is stored with Supabase in Singapore. It is encrypted in transit, and each account can read only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. We are based in the Philippines and handle personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app under Progress → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and backed-up progress. Purchase records may be kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for billing and tax. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; manage that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Children

Pawfer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. You can delete your account in the app, or email us for any other request. You can also contact your local data protection authority if you have a concern we haven't resolved.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
